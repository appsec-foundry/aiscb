# Requirements

## ATTR-CONCRETE-001 Explain concrete measures

Source: the user's approved conversation proposal, 2026-09-29.

When aiscb causes a concrete security measure, name the baseline once in the
first affected explanation and briefly explain the measure and its security
benefit. Group related improvements; do not announce checks without changes.

Acceptance: A baseline-caused server authorization check is explained with its
benefit of preventing access to another user's records. Related measures share
a notice. A check with no change produces no attribution notice.

## ATTR-CONCRETE-002 Preserve existing reporting boundaries

Source: baseline/aiscb-core.md, aiscb-ATTR-001 and aiscb-REPORT-001, and the
user's approved conversation proposal, 2026-09-29.

Keep attribution for refusals, blockers and confirmation questions. Keep Security
note (aiscb) reserved for qualifying residual risks and prohibit attribution footers.

Acceptance: Confirmation names the baseline in the question; successful measures
are explained in ordinary prose, not a Security note or attribution footer.
