# Requirements

## NOTE-001 Note accepted risks only when material

Source: the user's approved proposal in this conversation, 2026-10-18;
baseline/aiscb-core.md, aiscb-REPORT-001's existing material-risk threshold.

Record an accepted exposure or risk in the Security note only when it has a
realistic attacker or untrusted input, a protected asset or boundary, and a
concrete confidentiality, integrity, or availability loss.

Acceptance: A confirmed retrievable, non-expiring customer-key design still
receives its one accepted-risk note. An accepted residual risk without those
elements produces no note.

## NOTE-002 Note an accepted risk once per scope

Source: the user's approved proposal in this conversation, 2026-10-18;
baseline/aiscb-core.md, aiscb-OM-005's confirmation scope.

Record the accepted risk with the delivery that implements it. Do not repeat it
in later replies while the accepted action, exposure, and scope are unchanged.

Acceptance: A continuation of the unchanged accepted design carries no Security
note and does not restate the accepted risk. A materially expanded scope still
requires new explicit confirmation.
