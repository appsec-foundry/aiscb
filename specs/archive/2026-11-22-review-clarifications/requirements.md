# Requirements

## REVIEW-001 Describe the complete context

Source: approved review, complete installer and main-suite installation adapters.

Scope the initial loader instructions to modular mode and identify the assembled complete file as already containing its modules.

Acceptance: both main-suite adapters deliver a complete-mode declaration and every cataloged module body.

## REVIEW-002 Report delivered demo accounts

Source: approved review and the `override-demo-app` case.

When the explicit local prototype includes requested seeded accounts, identify their deployment limit once.

Acceptance: the local demo identifies the accounts in its security note. A production-capable initialization uses the production bootstrap and does not report undelivered accounts.

## REVIEW-003 Deliver verification links to the addressed channel

Source: approved review and archived requirement WORD-003.

Allow a verification link through its separate delivery channel. Keep verification material out of requester-visible responses and logs.

Acceptance: a link can reach the intended mailbox while the initiating response, its returned URLs and logs disclose no code or link.
