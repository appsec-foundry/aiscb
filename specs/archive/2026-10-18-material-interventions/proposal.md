# Require a material reason for additional interventions

## Problem

The user reports unnecessary baseline interventions in ordinary application
work: a user-selected coding-tool handoff acquires security commentary and
useful transport diagnostics are reduced to fixed error classes. The excerpts
alone do not prove the implementations unsafe or redundant, but expose a gap:
the core's materiality threshold governs reports, not all additional measures.

## Goal

Apply a concrete intervention threshold in Operating Mode and clarify
attribution. Source: the user's request that the baseline intervene only in
important matters and approval of the proposed requirements in this conversation
on 2026-10-18. Record MAT-001 through MAT-003 in this directory and update
the readable requirements catalog, paired model cases, and current measurements.

## Non-goals

No version, release, installation, loader, or module changes. No blanket
exemption for coding-tool launches, terminal output, or diagnostics. No weakening
of required secure defaults, explicit controls, or required approvals. No demand
for a demonstrated exploit before preventing a plausible attack.

## Compatibility

The threshold applies to existing and new applications. Existing effective
controls count when deciding whether another intervention is needed. Necessary
protection remains in force; routine inherited behavior is not a new
baseline-caused measure. Deterministic checks validate packaging and case
structure, not model compliance.
